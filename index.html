Executive summary

Where the catalog stands

Lomiiel’s catalog is partly administered and partly invisible to the mechanical system. Where a work is registered and his own imprint (Cuero Macho Records Publishing, administered by EMPIRE SF) or EMPIRE directly holds the claim, the infrastructure is in place: 57 of the 125 compositions where his writer identity is evidenced carry an attributable claim, and those compositions account for 542,223,775 plays, led by HAY LUPITA.

The other 68 compositions (233,819,391 associated plays) fall into three shapes. 56 recordings have no MLC work at all — almost all of them his own releases through Open Shift Distribution and Cuero Macho Records, including the entire July 2026 album. 10 recordings are registered with him on the writer line but every publisher claim on the work belongs to someone else, headed by PA QUE LO BAILES (BAILALO ROCKY) at 127,024,594 plays. And 7 recordings are registered without his name on the writer line even though the label’s own metadata credits him, headed by Me Gusta at 70 million plays.

Two structural issues sit underneath. His writer identity is split across nine separate MLC writer records (two with IPIs, seven without), and 15 registered works with his name carry zero recordings — several of them duplicates of works that do carry the recordings. Both are cheap to fix and both are prerequisites for any filing.

Zero compositions are ready to file today. That is by design: the report locates the gaps; split documentation establishes what he is entitled to claim. The document list at the end is what turns this analysis into filings.

Method

How this was built

The catalog was enumerated from the artist’s DSP discography (own releases plus a contributor-filtered search for appearances on other artists’ releases), giving 134 recordings with ISRCs and label attribution. Writer credits per recording were read from the label-supplied metadata behind each release. Spotify play counts were read from each track page individually on 2026-09-04. The MLC work universe was assembled from public-search sweeps on the recording artist name, on every legal-name spelling found in label metadata, on both writer IPIs, on each of the nine MLC writer records, and on his publishing imprint — 120 works, 78 of which list him as a writer — and every one of the 134 ISRCs was tested for linkage directly. Attribution reads the administrator layer of each publisher claim, treats his own imprint as his, and treats a publisher with an empty represented-writer list as his only where he is the sole writer on the work. Works by a different writer of similar name (Marco Antonio Santana Suárez, SGAE) were identified and excluded. PRO (Songview) data was not pulled for this edition and SoundExchange (master side) is outside its scope. Registration status is never payment status; account-level facts are an onboarding question, not a registry lookup.
